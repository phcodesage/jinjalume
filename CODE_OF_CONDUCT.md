# Code of Conduct

Jinjalume is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or technical opinion.

Expected behavior includes:

- being respectful and constructive;
- welcoming beginners and different viewpoints;
- focusing feedback on the work, not the person; and
- accepting responsibility and learning from mistakes.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without consent.

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed in confidence and handled fairly. Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
